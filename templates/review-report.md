> **Automatic remediation eligibility.** For each finding, the reviewer states whether it is eligible for one bounded automatic remediation cycle. If the field is omitted, ambiguous, or `uncertain`, the orchestrator treats the finding as ineligible and stops for human decision. Security, architecture, scope-expansion, and ambiguous findings must be marked `no`.
